About

Business Advisor.
Growth Partner.

For more than 27 years, I have helped organizations improve operations, adopt new technologies, increase revenue, and lead through periods of significant change — from Fortune 500 initiatives to privately owned businesses.

Luz Padilla-Niemann is a business transformation advisor, technology strategist, and operational leader with a track record of helping organizations at every stage of growth achieve measurable, lasting improvement. Her career spans business development, digital transformation, AI adoption, and enterprise-scale change management across industries.

Her deep roots in the automotive industry — where she rose to national leadership roles and earned recognition as a two-time Corporate Sales Manager of the Year — gave her a master class in operational complexity, technology adoption, and performance culture. That experience now powers a broader practice serving business owners, advisors, and organizations across multiple industries.

Business Advisor AI Consultant Technology Strategist Operational Excellence Growth Partner 2x Corporate Sales Manager of the Year Digital Retailing Certified STEM Advocate

Luz's journey began on the assembly line of a major automotive manufacturer — where she learned that great operations are built by understanding the work, respecting the people doing it, and building systems that allow teams to win. She rose steadily through the organization over 27+ years, ultimately leading national business development and digital transformation initiatives.

In one region she supported, five out of six client groups earned top performance recognition — a direct reflection of the operational strategy, performance culture, and leadership alignment she helped build.

Over time, Luz recognized a growing need beyond automotive: business owners across every industry were struggling to keep pace with rapid technological change while still trying to grow revenue and improve operations. That insight led to the creation of Wilder Tech.

Luz is also the founder of Wilder Ventures and The Next Chapter — a premium journal and keepsake collection for anyone navigating life's most meaningful transitions. A proud mother of two, a passionate mentor, and a strong advocate for STEM education and the next generation of leaders.
