About

Enterprise Customer Success & Strategic Account Leadership

I build strategic customer relationships, lead high-performing teams, and turn technology investments into measurable business results.

Throughout my career at General Motors, I've led digital transformation, enterprise account management, customer success, and revenue growth across complex dealer networks. My experience spans the full customer lifecycle — from implementation and onboarding to adoption, executive business reviews, retention, and expansion.

What sets me apart is the ability to connect strategy with execution. I work directly with executive stakeholders, understand their business challenges, and bring together sales, operations, technology, and product teams to deliver results.

Customer SuccessSoftware ImplementationAccount ManagementCustomer RetentionCustomer OnboardingDigital TransformationExecutive Stakeholder EngagementRevenue Expansion

My path started on the assembly line of a major automotive manufacturer, which taught me to understand the work, respect the people doing it, and build systems that let teams win.

I later founded Wilder Tech to bring the same adoption-first approach to growing businesses. I also mentor young professionals and champion STEM education, because technology is only as powerful as the people prepared to use it.
